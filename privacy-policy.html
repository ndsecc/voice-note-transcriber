Privacy Policy

Voice Note Transcriber for WhatsApp Web · Effective 1 November 2026

Voice Note Transcriber ("the extension") turns WhatsApp Web voice messages into text. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it goes and how you control it.

The short version

  • The extension has no servers and no accounts. We, the developer, never receive your data.
  • Voice message audio is sent only to the speech-to-text provider you choose, with your API key, only to transcribe it.
  • Transcripts, settings and your API key are stored only in your browser.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or sale of data.

The extension reads WhatsApp Web only in the tab where it runs, only to detect voice messages, transcribe them and build exports you request. It does not read other websites.

Third parties

Audio is sent to exactly one provider, the one you select in Settings:

  • Groq: groq.com/privacy-policy
  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • A custom endpoint you configure, for example your own Whisper server

You hold the account with that provider, and their terms and privacy policy apply to the audio they receive. The extension sends nothing anywhere else.

Your choices

  • Turn off automatic transcription in Settings to transcribe only when you click.
  • Delete all transcripts in Settings → Your data.
  • Export your transcripts as Markdown or JSON in Settings → Your data.
  • Remove the extension to delete everything it stored.

Played status in WhatsApp

To read a voice message's audio, the extension plays it silently in WhatsApp Web. WhatsApp therefore shows the message as played to its sender, just as when you listen to it yourself.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their data.
